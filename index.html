NEGRONI

Aperitivo • Stirred • Lowball

Equal parts gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth with an expressed orange peel.

Cocktail Spec

NEGRONI

Bitter-Sweet Balance • Florence, Italy c. 1919
GLASSWARE Rocks Glass
METHOD Stirred over ice
ICE Large clear cube
GARNISH Orange twist or wheel

AUTHENTIC SPECIFICATION

BARTENDER'S CRAFT TIP

Stir for 30–45 seconds until chilled and balanced. The goal is approximately 25% dilution to soften the bitter punch of Campari.

PROVENANCE & MYTH VS. FACT

Invented at Caffè Casoni when Count Camillo Negroni requested his Americano be strengthened with gin rather than soda water.
